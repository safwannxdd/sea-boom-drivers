Takeoff coding sheet (37 charts)

Takeoff = the first year of a dramatic, sustained increase: the start of the growth phase, not just any rise. Mark the year where the curve "breaks upward" and keeps going.

Choose already taken off before Y1 if it is clearly growing fast from the first point, and no takeoff if there is no clear break. Values are rescaled to 0–100 and the years are anonymised, so judge the shape only. Don't overthink each one; about 30 seconds per chart.
